Apply online for your Barangay Business Permit and Clearance

What would you like to apply for?

🏢

Applying for a Business Permit?

New or renewal Barangay Business Permit. Fee depends on your business group: ₱1,000 to ₱5,000.

📄

Applying for a Clearance?

Barangay Clearance: ₱50 to ₱250 depending on the kind. FREE for Indigency, Medical and Financial.

🌱

Applying for Barangay Clearance Pangkabuhayan?

FREE of charge. For NEW small businesses under the Pangkabuhayan program only.

How it works

  1. Choose what you are applying for and fill in the form.
  2. Attach your requirements (photo or PDF) and submit.
  3. Save your reference number (like 26-0001) and pay the fee if there is one.
  4. Claim your document at the Barangay Hall once it is approved.
